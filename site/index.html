Small mod.
Big convenience.

No macros running in the background. No guessing what the server said. Just a keybind, a sensible guard, and you in control.

⌨
01 / INPUT

Your key, your call

Hit R to send /rtpq exactly once. Change the bind any time from Minecraft’s Controls screen.

R
◷
02 / GUARD

Cooldown, not spam

A short local cooldown stops accidental repeats. Set it anywhere from zero to 30 seconds—or turn it off.

⌁
03 / FEEDBACK

Know it went through

Get an action-bar confirmation when the command is sent, or see how many seconds are left.

✓
✳

You stay in control. The mod never auto-queues or tries to read undocumented server messages. Your key press sends a regular command; server rules still apply.

Your cooldown.
Your rules.

On first launch, the mod creates config/rtpq.properties. Edit the two settings, save, then restart Minecraft.
